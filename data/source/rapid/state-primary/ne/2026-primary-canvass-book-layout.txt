                                        Reported Problems
Washington County
Shortly after early voting ballots were issued, Washington County identified that the Learning Community
Coordinating Council (LCCC) Subcouncil 1 contest had been mistakenly included on all ballot styles in the county
instead of only the four ballot styles eligible to vote in that contest. The county promptly corrected the issue by
creating revised ballot styles for affected voters.

The LCCC Subcouncil 1 contest included four candidates vying for four nominations. Because all candidates were
nominated regardless of vote totals, the ballot style error did not affect the outcome of the election.
